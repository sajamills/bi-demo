    Fan identity resolution (Fan 360)
    The same fan shows up in ticketing, League+, the shop and the fantasy app under different IDs and slightly different emails. Matching them into one golden record is what makes fan counts, churn and lifetime value trustworthy.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Match funnel records → golden fans, rule by rule
    
        
    Why it matters only visible once identities are joined
    
      
    
      
    Sample matches auto-merged · confidence ≥ 0.90
    
      
    Steward review queue confidence 0.70–0.90 · a person decides
    
      
    Duplicate golden records already in dim_fan found by the "Fan contact email is unique" quality check
    
      
    Synthetic records; match quality is measured against the synthetic ground truth. In production: AWS Entity Resolution (rule-based + ML matching) writing to a Gold identity graph, with steward review in the catalog.
